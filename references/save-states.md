# Save states: the run is resumable and handoff-able at every boundary

The bundle plus its `state.json` IS the save state. Share it at any boundary and a
different person or agent — on a different machine, a different token budget — reads
the state and continues. Manage transitions with `scripts/state.py` (init / show /
advance / reopen); every transition records actor, timestamp, and note, so the state
history is itself part of the process trace.

Why states are named: each name answers *what is true now* — what's been captured,
who produced it, what it unlocks, and why someone would deliberately stop there.
Stopping is not failure; it's the mechanism that lets a builder spend thirty minutes
and hand the expensive synthesis to someone else's agent.

## The ladder

**`negotiated`** — the host, runtime, capabilities, authority, roots, constraints, and
unresolved environment questions captured in `environment.json`.
- Produced by: the operator plus the read-only environment probe.
- Unlocks: a bounded, portable observation contract without exposing target evidence.
- Why stop here: the host needs additional authority or an evidence-root decision
  before blind elicitation and scanning can be scoped honestly.

**`elicited`** — the builder's blind beliefs, captured as Q&A data points.
- Produced by: the person who runs it, with the target's builder. Cheap — questions
  only, no scans. **Required, and required first**: blind capture is unrecoverable.
  Once the builder has seen any scan output, their unanchored model is gone for good —
  no later phase can retake this measurement.
- Unlocks: everything downstream can now compute deltas against a belief snapshot.
- Why stop here: you only had the builder for twenty minutes.

**`exported`** — scans run, every evidence file excerpted into `exports/`, graphs and
run blocks written, manifest complete.
- Produced by: mostly machine time, low judgment. The builder need not be present.
- Unlocks: any agent anywhere can reconcile from this — the evidence travels.
- Why stop here: this is the natural handoff point for a builder who doesn't want to
  spend tokens investigating — elicit + export is their whole contribution.

**`shared`** — bundle at a reachable destination (repo, page, drive); secret gate
passed (hard: unresolved warnings block this transition).
- Produced by: whoever holds the bundle, via the Share phase mechanics.
- Unlocks: cross-person handoff. Before `shared`, only the local machine can continue;
  after it, anyone with access can.
- Why stop here: the QA-manager case exactly — "here's my history, graph, and files;
  your agent picks up the rest."

**`reconciled`** — deltas computed (belief vs evidence, per slot) and interrogated as
far as the available people allow.
- Produced by: the receiving agent, expensive judgment work. Deltas only the builder
  can resolve become a **question list — itself a mini save state** that travels back
  to the builder (minutes of their time), whose answers advance the state on return.
- Unlocks: card filling with both kinds of evidence in hand.
- Why stop here: waiting on the builder's answers to the question list.

**`carded`** — every slot terminal, replication test applied, Tending written.
- Produced by: the receiving agent, with reconciled material.
- Why stop here: awaiting owner review before the deck goes anywhere.

**`delivered`** — the finished deck re-shared to its audience (the shareability slot's
answer), destination recorded.

## Rules of progression

- Forward is the default: negotiated → elicited → exported → shared → reconciled → carded →
  delivered. `state.py` enforces order and records who advanced what, when, and why.
- Cycles reopen, they don't erase: a reconcile that triggers a rescan reopens
  `exported` with a reason; history keeps every transition, so the path (including
  how many cycles a concern cost) stays legible in the trace.
- Skipping `elicited` is the one destructive shortcut. Every other state can be redone
  later at the price of tokens; blind belief cannot. If the builder is truly
  unavailable, record `elicited: skipped(reason)` explicitly — the deltas downstream
  then measure evidence against nothing, and the card must say so.
- A handoff is just: share the bundle at any state, point the receiving agent at
  `state.json`. The receiving agent trusts the state description, not assumptions —
  it reads what's pending, not what it guesses was done.
- **A bundle must carry its review surfaces before it travels.** `README.md` (human
  synopsis: where it stands, what was found, what needs review, what's next) and
  `handoff.json` (typed actions for the next agent) are generated by
  `scripts/bundle_synopsis.py` and required by `state.py` before any advance to
  `shared`. Regenerate them whenever the bundle's contents change — a stale synopsis
  misleads exactly the reader it exists for.
